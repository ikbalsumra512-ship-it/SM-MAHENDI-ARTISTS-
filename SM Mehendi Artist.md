## Caring for Your Mehendi

General tips to help your design look its best. Ask on WhatsApp for guidance on your specific pattern.

### Let It Rest

Keep the paste on for as long as you comfortably can so the colour develops well.

### Remove Gently

Flake the dried paste off with your fingers rather than washing it away with water.

### Keep It Moisturised

Dab a little natural oil over the design once the paste is removed.

### Avoid Scrubbing

Be gentle around the design for the first day and avoid harsh soaps.
